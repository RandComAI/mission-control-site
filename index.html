Follow one real workflow

One project. Three ways to stay in control.

Start on your Mac, leave the desk, and keep directing the same work from another AI session. RandComp does not create three copies of the task.

Example goal: “Launch a small online store. Have Product choose the scope, Developer build it, and QA verify it.”

1

At your Mac

See the whole project in the desktop app.

Start with one sentence about what you want. Your project's coordinator turns it into a plan of focused tasks and hires the helpers to do them. Every task shows who is on it, what it produced, and what needs you.

Desktop app · visual control
2

Away from the desk

Check on your team from anywhere.

Your team keeps working even when you're away. From any computer, a couple of simple commands show what needs you and let you answer. Rather not touch commands at all? Just ask your AI assistant to check in — it can see your whole team and pass your answers along.

Remote check-in · low bandwidth

The project, team, messages, and results are the same ones shown in the desktop app.

The hidden cost of the agent army

Use one agent when one is enough.

More agents are not automatically better. The hidden trap is token cost: every session reads context, and coordination consumes more. Keep simple work in one focused session; bring in a team when parallel work or independent verification earns its cost.

Small, isolated work

Improve this HTML page.

One fresh Frontend session may be faster and cheaper than five agents rereading the same project. No planning ceremony or unnecessary handoffs.

The difference

One assistant, or a whole team?

A single AI writes the code — then grades its own homework. RandComp works like a real company: the developer builds it, and a separate QA who never saw the developer's reasoning checks it independently.

One AI assistant

Does everything itself.

Writes the code, then reviews its own work — same context, same blind spots. Everything you ask shares one crowded conversation. And it only works with one company's model, in one company's cloud.

writeschecks its own workhopes for the best

A RandComp team

Specialists who check each other.

A coordinator plans the work. A developer builds it. An independent QA verifies it — a fresh pair of AI eyes that never saw the developer's reasoning. Everything runs on your Mac, with whichever AI you choose.

Why the name?

About RandComp

I wanted to build fun things without a team — so I hired one: a coordinator, designers, developers, QA. They're all agents. I couldn't think of a name for the company either, so I called it Random Company — out of laziness. RandComp is just the shortcut.
